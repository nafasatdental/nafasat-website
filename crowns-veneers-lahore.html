Crowns and veneers in Lahore

A crown covers and protects a weak, broken or root treated tooth. A veneer is a thin shell on the front of a tooth that changes its shape or shade. We offer zirconia and E-max crowns, and composite and E-max veneers, matched to your own teeth.

Is it right for you?

Who it helps

  • A broken or heavily filled tooth
  • A tooth after root canal treatment
  • Chipped, worn or uneven front teeth
  • Gaps or stains that whitening cannot fix

Step by step

What happens

You will know each step before it happens, and you can stop and ask at any point.

  1. Consultation and shade

    We plan the shape and match the shade to your neighbouring teeth, and you approve it.

  2. Preparing the tooth

    A thin layer is reshaped under local anaesthesia so the crown or veneer sits flush.

  3. Temporary

    You wear a temporary while the lab makes yours.

  4. Fitting

    We check fit, colour and bite before bonding it in place.

  5. Review

    A short check makes sure your bite feels natural.

Aftercare

  • Avoid sticky and very hard food on a temporary crown.
  • Sensitivity after the tooth is prepared is common and settles.
  • Brush and floss around your crown like a natural tooth.
  • If the bite feels high or the temporary comes loose, call us.
  • Keep your six monthly check-ups and cleaning.

Good to know

  • Some tooth surface is reshaped, and this cannot be undone.
  • A temporary crown is worn while the lab makes yours, so avoid sticky food on it.
  • Sensitivity after preparation is common and settles.
  • A prepared tooth may later need root canal treatment.
  • Shade is matched as closely as possible to your own teeth.

Good to know

Crowns and veneers, answered

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

Zirconia or E-max, which should I choose?

Zirconia is very strong and suits back teeth that take heavy chewing. E-max lets more light through, so it often looks more natural on front teeth. We advise you tooth by tooth.

How long do crowns last?

With good care and check-ups crowns usually last many years. Grinding, decay at the edges and accidents are what shorten their life.

Composite or E-max veneers?

Composite veneers are quicker, cost less and can be repaired. E-max veneers resist staining and keep their shine for longer.

Will my tooth be shaved down?

A small amount of surface is reshaped so the crown or veneer is not bulky. This cannot be undone, so we only do it when it is the right treatment.
