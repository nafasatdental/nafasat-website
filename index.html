Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 4 PM to 10 PM. Sunday we are closed. We work by appointment, so message us first and we hold a time for you.

Where exactly is Nafasat?

Plot 110-C, Main Boulevard, NFC Society Phase 1, opposite Central Park, on the first floor. It is on the same row as Pizza Hut NFC, near the roundabout where NFC meets Valencia Town.

How do I find out what my treatment costs?

Build your plan on the website or send us a message on WhatsApp. We reply with your exact price and any founding discount the same evening. Nothing starts until you have agreed to the plan and its cost.

How can I pay?

Cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. You receive a numbered invoice for every visit.

How do you keep instruments clean?

Every instrument is cleaned, sterilised and sealed in our sterilisation room before each patient. Single use items are used once and discarded.

Do you see children?

Yes. We go at your child's pace and explain everything in simple words, and every child who has treatment goes home with a small gift and a Brave Smile Award.

Who are the visiting consultants?

Specialists in oral surgery, gums, root canals, prosthodontics and orthodontics who see patients at Nafasat by appointment. Dr Hamza arranges the visit when your case needs one.
