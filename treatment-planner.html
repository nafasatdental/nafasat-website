    Good to know

    Pricing, answered

    Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

    Why is there no rate list here?

    Because a price only means something once we know your case. Send your plan and we reply with your exact price, so there are no surprises in the chair.

    What is the founding discount?

    A thank you for patients who come to us in our first months. It is added to your price when you message us on WhatsApp.

    Do I have to book after I get my price?

    No. Your plan and price are yours to keep. Book when you are ready.

    How quickly will you reply?

    Monday to Saturday between 4 and 10 PM, usually the same evening. Messages sent later are answered the next evening.
