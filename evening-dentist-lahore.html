Good to know

Evening dentist near you, answered

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 4 PM to 10 PM. Sunday closed. We work by appointment, so message us and we hold a time for you.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is plenty of parking at the building on Main Boulevard.

Can I book for the same evening?

Often, yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we offer the earliest free time.

Which treatments do you offer in the evening?

Everything: check-ups, scaling, fillings, root canal treatment, crowns, veneers, implants, braces, aligners, whitening, dentures and children's dentistry.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa, with a numbered invoice for every visit.
