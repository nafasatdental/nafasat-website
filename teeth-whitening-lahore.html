Is it right for you?

Who it helps

  • Yellowing from tea, coffee, smoking or age
  • Before a wedding or a big event
  • Teeth that are healthy, with any decay or gum problems treated first

Step by step

What happens

You will know each step before it happens, and you can stop and ask at any point.

  1. Check-up

    We make sure your teeth and gums are healthy enough to whiten and note your starting shade.

  2. Scaling and polishing

    Removing tartar and surface stain first lets the gel work evenly.

  3. Protecting the gums

    A barrier covers the gums so the gel only touches the teeth.

  4. Whitening

    The gel is applied in short rounds in one visit, or you whiten at home with custom trays.

  5. Keeping it bright

    Simple habits and a yearly touch-up keep the shade for longer.

Aftercare

  • Mild sensitivity for a day or two is normal.
  • For 48 hours after whitening, skip tea, coffee, colas and dark sauces.
  • Use a soft brush, and sensitivity toothpaste if needed.
  • Slight gum tenderness after scaling settles quickly.
  • Book your next scaling in six months.

Good to know

  • Sensitivity for a day or two is common.
  • Crowns, fillings and veneers do not change colour.
  • Results vary and fade slowly with tea, coffee and smoking.
  • Not advised during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Gums may feel tender for a short while.

Good to know

Teeth whitening, answered

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

Is professional whitening safe for my teeth?

Yes, when it is done or supervised by a dentist with professional gel. Short-term sensitivity is the most common side effect and it passes within a day or two.

How long does whitening last?

It fades slowly, faster with tea, coffee, colas and smoking. Many people keep the result with a touch-up every year or so.

Will whitening change the colour of my crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only works on natural enamel, so we plan around any visible crowns or fillings.

Can I whiten my teeth during pregnancy?

We advise waiting until after pregnancy and breastfeeding.
